The problem

Addis Ababa's delivery market wasn't built for how the city actually moves.

Three overlapping failures push small traders back into taxis and leave thousands of daily commuters travelling with empty hands.

Exorbitant, volatile pricing

A single 100-gram document from Piassa to Bole costs 250–400 birr — completely disconnected from its value, and out of reach for small online vendors clearing 100–200 birr in profit.

Traffic & empty trips

Tens of thousands of commuters cross the city empty-handed every day, while hundreds of extra motorbikes are added purely to move goods — worsening congestion and emissions.

One-size-fits-all

A 50-birr letter and an 80,000-birr laptop travel the same way, with the same risk. There's no tiered pricing, security, or accountability to match the value of what's being sent.

The solution

One route‑matching engine. Four purpose‑built tiers.

Every order is priced and routed by what it is, not treated the same as everything else — from a forgotten key to a full office relocation.

How it works

Three taps to ship. Three taps to earn.

Sending something

  1. 1

  1. 1

Security & chain of custody

Trust, built into every handoff.

Five safeguards address the three questions every new user asks: what if it breaks, what if it's lost, and what if I don't know who's carrying it.

  1. Fayda Digital ID verified

    Every courier is registered against Ethiopia's national biometric ID before their first delivery.

  2. Item value held in escrow

    For higher‑value items, the equivalent amount is held in the courier's in‑app wallet until delivery is confirmed.

  3. Photo / video at pickup

    A timestamped photo or short video records the item's condition before it ever leaves the sender's hands.

  4. Live GPS tracking

    Sender and recipient both watch the delivery move in real time, end to end.

  5. 4‑digit OTP confirmation

    Funds release only once the recipient enters the one‑time code sent to their phone — proof the right person received it.

  6. Instant payout

    82% of the fare lands in the courier's wallet immediately — the platform's 18% commission and 2% withholding tax are deducted automatically.
