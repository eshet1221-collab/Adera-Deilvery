About us

Built for how Addis Ababa actually moves.

Loyal Delivery Movers turns the commutes already happening across the city into a delivery network — matching parcels, documents, and cargo with pedestrians, cyclists, and drivers already heading that way.

Our mission

Make sending something across Addis Ababa affordable and reliable for everyone — not just those who can pay premium courier rates — by putting the city's existing foot and vehicle traffic to work.

How we work

No fleet, no warehouses. Every delivery is carried by someone already going that way, matched by route and verified at every handoff — so the cost stays low without cutting corners on trust.

Who it's for

Small traders and online sellers who need affordable delivery, everyday commuters who want to earn on a route they already travel, and anyone who just needs something to get across town.
